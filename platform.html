Platform architecture

Three engines. One decision layer.

PostAxiom is being built as a modular enterprise system. The product surface should stay simple, but the internal logic must be able to switch between forecasting, causal estimation, and action support depending on the actual business question.

1 — Forecast Engine

Forecasting for unstable business systems

  • Many-series and hierarchical forecasting
  • Global ML models for large business data
  • Break-aware and regime-aware model selection
  • Scenario-conditioned forecasts
  • Probabilistic intervals with calibration
  • Linked-series systems for demand, price, supply, and revenue
2 — Causal Engine

Policy-effect estimation for messy enterprise data

  • A/B, geo, and operational experiment analysis
  • Observational causal workflows
  • DML and orthogonal-score pipelines
  • Elasticity estimation and promotion lift
  • Event studies and policy evaluation
  • Counterfactual simulation under uncertainty
3 — Decision Engine

From models to usable business action

  • Scenario memos and recommendation summaries
  • Monitoring, alerts, and model-change explanations
  • Action logs and approval workflows
  • Human-in-the-loop decision support
  • Executive-ready communication instead of raw model dumps
  • Governed rollout of policy changes

How the engines work together

The same business problem can move across layers. A revenue question may start as forecasting, become a pricing elasticity problem, and end as a monitored policy rollout. The platform is meant to recognize that these are connected tasks rather than separate software purchases.

Forecast first

Estimate likely outcomes under current conditions, identify drift, and surface instability before users act.

Estimate the intervention

When the question becomes “what if we do X,” move from prediction to causal effect estimation with appropriate assumptions and uncertainty.

Support the rollout

Translate estimates into operational recommendations, holdouts, monitoring, and reviewable decision artifacts.

Design principle: the core moat is not a generic agent. It is a system that knows when the problem is forecasting, when it is causal, and when it has become a business decision under uncertainty.
