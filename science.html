Scientific core

Serious methods where they matter.

The product surface should feel clear and usable. The science underneath should be deep. PostAxiom is designed to combine modern machine learning, time-series econometrics, and causal inference without turning the product into a method zoo.

Modern forecasting

Many-series forecasting, global ML models, dynamic regression, probabilistic intervals, and nowcasting for enterprise environments.

Structural instability

Break diagnostics, change-point logic, break-aware retraining, and regime-sensitive selection when business relationships shift.

Linked economic systems

Cointegration-aware and system-level modeling for variables that move together over time instead of being forecast in isolation.

Causal rigor

DML, doubly robust methods, DiD, event studies, synthetic controls, IV, and experiment analysis where feasible.

What differentiates the science direction

The goal is not to advertise test names to customers. The goal is to encode the right scientific response to the right empirical problem, then present that response in business language.

Break-aware forecasting

When a business series has changed regime, the platform should know that a pre-break model is no longer the right anchor. Structural break detection, instability diagnostics, and adaptive retraining are central here.

System-aware modeling

Large companies do not have one series. They have families of related series. Prices, demand, spend, channel mix, and supply variables move together and should often be modeled jointly.

Policy-aware estimation

Not every forecasting question is causal. But once a user asks what happens if they change price, launch a campaign, or reallocate budget, the platform must move into credible policy-effect estimation.

Method families we intend to support

This is a practical science stack, not an academic catalogue. The methods below are included because they map to real enterprise decisions.

Forecasting and time-series
  • Benchmarks such as ARIMA and Prophet where useful
  • Global ML forecasting for many related series
  • State-space and dynamic regression approaches
  • Volatility and risk forecasting for unstable series
  • Cointegration and system-level time-series models
  • Structural break and regime-shift logic
Causal inference and policy analysis
  • Experimental analysis for randomized and quasi-randomized interventions
  • Observational causal methods for enterprise data
  • DML and doubly robust estimation
  • Difference-in-differences and event studies
  • Synthetic controls and panel causal methods
  • Instrumental variables when endogeneity is material
Product rule: the customer should see better forecasts, more credible scenarios, and clearer decisions. The sophisticated econometrics and ML live underneath that experience.
