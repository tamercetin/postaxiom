Beyond static models. Beyond black-box prediction. Beyond yesterday’s assumptions.

PostAxiom is a standalone startup platform for forecasting, causal inference, and decision support. The product direction combines modern machine learning, serious econometrics, and policy-aware decision systems for enterprises operating under structural instability, regime shifts, and high-dimensional observational data.

What “post-axiom” means

The old enterprise stack assumes stable regimes, simple correlations, and reusable formulas for every decision. PostAxiom starts where those assumptions break. We treat forecasting, causal inference, and policy simulation as one connected problem.

Post-forecast-onlyNot just point estimates. Probabilistic, scenario-conditioned, and decision-value forecasts.
Post-correlationNot just prediction. Experimental and observational causal estimation for business action.
Post-static-regimeNot just one stable world. Break-aware and linked-system models under change.
Illustrative output
Forecast layerSegment-level demand softens after a regime shift, so post-break models dominate the historical benchmark.
Causal layerElasticity estimates from observational data suggest room for a controlled price increase with uncertainty still inside tolerance.
Decision layerIncrease price by 2–3% in monitored units, preserve holdouts, and update the policy weekly as evidence arrives.

A new enterprise category

Most tools either forecast, dashboard, or automate. PostAxiom is being designed to do something harder: estimate what is likely to happen, estimate what would happen if you act, and convert that into a usable decision workflow.

Forecast better

Many-series forecasting, probabilistic intervals, break-aware model selection, and linked-series systems built for real business data.

Decide with causality

DML, DiD, event studies, synthetic controls, IV, and experiment analysis for pricing, promotions, growth, and policy questions.

Operate with confidence

Scenario memos, executive summaries, monitoring, action logs, and human-in-the-loop decision support rather than blind automation.

Initial commercial wedge: U.S. enterprises with large multi-series data in pricing, demand planning, revenue forecasting, marketing measurement, and operational policy analysis.
